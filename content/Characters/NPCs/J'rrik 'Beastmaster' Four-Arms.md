J'rrik "Beastmaster" Four-Arms was a [[Zorbrak]] *Protostalker* [[Tracer]] working as a mercenary, hired by the [[Red Suns]], and was deployed outside the [[Red Suns Warehouse]] on the galactic calendar day of `27/06/741 3e`. He survived an altercation with [[The Outrunners|the Outrunners]] at this location, but was found dead later that night on the 150th floor of [[Twilyt Tower]], alongside his pet brood swarmer, "Princess".

This unique mercenary was known for his combat technique that saw him fighting alongside his two brood swarmer companions, and later alongside the matriarch of that brood, "Princess".
