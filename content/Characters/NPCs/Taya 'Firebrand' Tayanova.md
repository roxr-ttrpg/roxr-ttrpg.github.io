Taya "Firebrand" Tayanova was an [[Oozoid]] *Quickdraw* [[Scoundrel]] working as a mercenary, hired by the [[Red Suns]], and was in command of the [[Red Suns Mod Shop]] on the galactic calendar day of `27/06/741 3e`, where she was critically injured during an altercation with [[The Outrunners|the Outrunners]], before being interrogated. She was rushed to an undisclosed Red Suns medical facility that same day, where she was immediately provided a temporary bionic leg replacement, as it would take too long for her ooze to reconstitute. She was redeployed that same night onto the 150th floor of [[Twilyt Tower]], where she was found dead and decapitated.
