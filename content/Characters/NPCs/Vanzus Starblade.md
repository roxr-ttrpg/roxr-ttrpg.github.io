Vanzus Starblade was the [[Nilari]] *Symbiote* [[Esper]] who was the last CEO of [[Twilyt Energistics]] prior to its corporate takeover from the [[Kyojin Energy Company]]. His death is linked to a number of other occurrences from the galactic calendar day of `27/06/741 3e`, in which [[The Outrunners|the Outrunners]] infiltrated and attacked [[Twilyt Tower]], eventually making their way to the 150th floor where Starblade's office was located. 

>*He was an unpleasant fella. 
>The galaxy is better off without men like him.*
- ***[[Giff Bigstar]], in a Post-Operation interview***

While Vanzus had a profitable career in his 20 years leading [[Twilyt Energistics]], his true famed breakthrough: the discovery of [[Abyssal Extract]], was discovered near the end of his career. While Vanzus Starblade claimed many great things about this Extract, most other companies, including much of the rest of management within Twilyt Energistics, were cautious of this discovery. As a result, Starblade had a hard time finding people willing to help him in this venture, eventually resorting to the aid of the infamous [[Red Suns]] of [[New Paradise]]. 

After his death, an autopsy of Vanzus revealed that he had highly lethal levels of [[Abyssal Extract]] running through his bloodstream, alongside early onset sights of [[Discordant|Entropic Discordance]].
