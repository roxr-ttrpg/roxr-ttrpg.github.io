█████ ████████ is a [[Raizo]] *Renegade* [[Enforcer]] with ties to the infamous [[Kuzai Syndicate]]'s house on the planet [[Ferrox]], and is one of [[The Outrunners|the Outrunners]] who were hired by [[Giff Bigstar]] to take down the [[Red Suns]]. While not currently initiated in the syndicate, she still takes jobs on their behalf. Currently, █████ is seeking a mark on the planet [[Kleio Prime]]; an individual who betrayed the syndicate-affiliated family of [[New Paradise]].

>*I'm told this up-and-coming muscle for hire will be useful to our mission. She seems like too much of a risk, but the Syndicate insists on her involvement.*
- ***[[Giff Bigstar]]'s Datapad***

- **"Deez"**

Despite now being closer to organized crime than street gangs, growing up alongside the [[Chemtek Revolution]] movements of [[Ferrox]] led █████ towards adopting the scrappier, more improvised brawling fighter styles of the junkyard planet.
