Nyarle is a [[Benthos]] **GRIDRUNNER**, and one of [[The Outrunners|the Outrunners]] who were hired by [[Giff Bigstar]] to take down the [[Red Suns]]. Despite being a Benthos, Nyarle was lost in transport and grew up on the planet [[Astrovega]], which she calls her homeworld. She became a *Gridrunner* in order to make ends meet on the technological face of the planet, but the ancient Eldest ruins found on Astrovega have also been a point of curiosity for Nyarle.

>*Infiltrating a gang this organized will require the skills of a competent Gridrunner. Nyarle will be instrumental to our success.*
- *[[Giff Bigstar]]'s Datapad*
