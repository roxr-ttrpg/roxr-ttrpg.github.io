Malalim Dagat is a [[Murexian]] serving the [[the Church of Karkosa|Church of Karkosa]] as a *Voidcaller* [[Oracle]], and one of [[The Outrunners|the Outrunners]] who were hired by [[Giff Bigstar]] to take down the [[Red Suns]]. They were brought far from their homeworld of [[Kharabis]] to the planet of [[Kleio Prime]], following a tip from the Church of Karkosa that an [[The Relic|Eldest relic]] had been located on the planet.

>*An archivist from the Church of Karkosa... doesn't have the most experience, but hopefully they can shine a light on the mystery of that artefact.*
- ***[[Giff Bigstar]]'s Datapad***

![[Malalim.png|300]]
- **Malalim Dagat**

While at the [[Red Suns Warehouse]], Malalim felt their connection with [[The Abyss|the Abyss]] suddenly deepen, and while it went unnoticed at first this connection momentarily manifested later that same day, during an altercation with [[Akira Kadashi]] and a number of [[Red Suns]] soldiers.
