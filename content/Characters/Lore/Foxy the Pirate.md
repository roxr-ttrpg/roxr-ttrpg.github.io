Foxy the Pirate was one of the mass-produced animatronic mascots created by [[Fazbear Entertainment]], and is one of the main four cast members at most [[Freddy Fazbear's Pizzeria]] locations. Unlike the other three animatronics, he was often posted by himself on a side-stage called "Pirate's Cove". Like the rest of Fazbear Entertainment's animatronics after the corporation's bankruptcy, most known Foxy models have been left to wither and decay as they lie abandoned in pizzeria locations galaxy-wide. 

Despite being relegated to a side stage, Foxy the Pirate was one of the most popular animatronic designs utilized by [[Fazbear Entertainment]] during the corporation's peak.
