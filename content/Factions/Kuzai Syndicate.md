>The Kuzai Syndicate is the biggest crime organization in the galaxy, choosing to get involved in legitimate businesses as a palatable front as they conduct their more nefarious operations from the shadows. They are bold enough to operate in the territory of every faction, even those with the most burdensome laws, unafraid of those who would try to stop them. They believe in loyalty above all else, and those who are initiated into the family receive mystical tattoos imbued with the power of the somber aspect of the Harmony.
- *The Outrunner's Handbook, vol 1.*
