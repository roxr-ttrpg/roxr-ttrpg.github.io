Discordants are members of the four Discordant species, associated with each of the four Tones of [[Discordant Tones|Discord]]. While distinct in their effects and transformations, these four species all act in much the same way, either being passed down hereditarily and often considered a "family curse" when done so, or twisting and corrupting an individual of a non-Discordant lineage at some point in their life, for any number of reasons. 

Additionally, each member of a Discordant species must, at some point in their lives, make a subconscious decision that will decide their place in the galaxy: either embrace the [[Discordant Tones|Discordant Tone]] and be turned entirely into a [[Fiend]] of [[Cacophony]], or reject the Tone and retain the humanity that remains.

Discordant individuals are, still, individual and as such develop their own factions and communities. Often, these factions come into conflict with each other - both between two different [[Discordant Tones]], or fighting within a singular Tone. 
##### The Four Discordant Species:
- [[Dathu]], Discordants of the [[Discordant Tones|Tone]] of [[Instinct]]. Transformed by feelings of wrath and violent outbursts, corruption manifests in the Dathu as exaggerated insectile traits, and connection to a mysterious discordant hivemind. 
- [[Motlien|Motliens]], Discordants of the [[Discordant Tones|Tone]] of [[Thrill]]. Seeking constant sensory overload, Thrill manifests in Motliens through growing, twisted clown-like features and explosive personalities that often break into uncontrollable fits of laughter.
- [[Terminox]], Discordants of the [[Discordant Tones|Tone]] of [[Progress]]. Denying human emotion, Terminox are charmed by the idea of Progress into ever-improvement. This often twists their bodies into robotic, bio-mechanical forms built from hellish metal and cables of flesh.
- [[Varg]], Discordants of the [[Discordant Tones|Tone]] of [[Entropy]]. Corruption manifests in these afflicted individuals as a vampiric curse, ever wanting to fill the emptiness inside themselves with the essence and emotions of the unafflicted. 
##### Notable Discordants:
- [[Vermina Sanguinar]], a [[Varg]] revolutionary currently working for [[Giff Bigstar]] as a *Holostar* [[Icon]]. Discordance not hereditary, [[Entropy]] rejected and humanity retained.
