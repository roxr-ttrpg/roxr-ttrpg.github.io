>*While the Stardust Rhapsody is filled with grand galactic factions, planetary governments, and interstellar organizations, many people chart their own course throughout the stars and make their way through the galaxy individually or in small groups. 
>
>These wayfarers are called Outrunners.*
- ***The Outrunner's Handbook, vol 1.***

The Outrunners are the group who were initially hired by [[Giff Bigstar]] to take down the [[Red Suns]]. 

The group currently consists of 5 members:
- [[Deez]], a [[Raizo]] [[Enforcer]]
- [[Headstrong]], an [[Anitron]] [[Juggernaut]]
- [[Malalim Dagat]], a [[Murexian]] [[Oracle]]
- [[Nyarle]], a [[Benthos]] [[Gridrunner]]
- [[Splat]], an [[Oozoid]] [[Soldier]]
