Freddy Fazbear's Pizzeria was an interstellar chain of family diners spanning the galaxy, before its eventual bankruptcy and closure in ████. At it's peak, the chain had ███ locations open to the public. Despite it's incredible scale, the business had a hard time repeatedly meeting annual target goals, especially in the years leading up to the bankruptcy, due in part to the diner's primary novelty: bespoke animatronic entertainers. 

In the years before the closure, [[Fazbear Entertainment]], the pizzeria's parent corporation, tried many ways to keep these novelties alive while cutting back on operating costs. Initial attempts to purely reduce animatronic maintenance costs only resulted in a loss of appeal as their mascots seemed to decay over the months, and later resulted in an increase in injuries as the machines started to become "Quirky at night". Secondary attempts resulted in the invention of the [[Springlock Suit|"Springlock Suit"]], which could be worn and piloted by people, rather than the endoskeletons Fazbear Entertainment utilized in the past. These Springlock suits were deemed unsafe for workers, and it is widely believed that a wave of related lawsuits resulted in the bankruptcy of the pizzeria chain and its parent corporation.
