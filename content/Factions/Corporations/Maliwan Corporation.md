Maliwan is a corporation affiliated with the [[Nexus Consortium]]. The corporation is currently under the leadership of CEO ██████ ███████, and their central headquarters are located on the planet ██████.

Maliwan produce goods for a number of economic sectors, including general goods, consumer electronics and private military.

![[Security Bot.png|300]]
- A *Maliwan* **Security Bot**

The interplanetary space station [[Research Station KlS-01]] was owned and operated by the Maliwan Corporation prior to the station crash landing on the planet of [[Kleio Prime]]. The [[The Outrunners|Outrunner]] [[Splat]] was an employee stationed on this research station, before being subjugated to the station's Oozoid Mecha-Soldier program.
