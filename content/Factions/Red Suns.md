The Red Suns are a gang operating from the city of [[New Paradise]], but who have territory across the planet of [[Kleio Prime]]. Their current goals are unknown. They employ a range of combat tactics, including coordinated footsoldiers, hired mercenaries and salvaged [[Maliwan Corporation|Maliwan]] security bots. It has also been discovered that the Red Suns are trying to proliferate and distribute chemicals that use a compound known as [[Abyssal Extract|"Abyssal Extract"]]. High ranking members of the Red Suns have combat stims using this substance, which they employ the use of in battle. 

![[Red Suns Footsoldier.png|300]]
- **A** ***Red Suns*** **Footsoldier** (identified ***"Deez"***)

After the crash landing of [[Research Station KlS-01]], the Red Suns were able to salvage most of the technology from the wreckage. It is speculated that this was the central reason for their sudden increase in violent activity.
It is also known that the Red Suns are in the possession of an [[The Relic|Eldest relic]], originally found by archaeologists under the [[One Great Sea]]. This relic is also speculated to be a source of the Red Suns' growing power.

- [[Akira Kadashi]], a [[Raizo]] [[Juggernaut]] and ex-[[Kuzai Syndicate]] member.
- [[J'rrik 'Beastmaster' Four-Arms|J'rrik "Beastmaster" Four-Arms]], a [[Zorbrak]] [[Tracer]], and hired mercenary.
- [[Taya 'Firebrand' Tayanova|Taya "Firebrand" Tayanova]], an [[Oozoid]] [[Scoundrel]], and hired mercenary.
##### Associated Locations:
- [[77th Street]], a main street of [[New Paradise]] often blockaded by the Red Suns and used as a route for various supply convoys.
- [[Red Suns Mod Shop|"The Mod Shop"]], a safehouse captained by the mercenary [[Taya 'Firebrand' Tayanova|"Firebrand"]], and used by the Red Suns to modify civilian gravcars with various weapons and armor.
- [[Red Suns Warehouse|"The Warehouse"]], an under-construction facility nestled underneath the mountains of the badlands of  [[Kleio Prime]]. 
-  [[Red Suns Electronics Facility|"The Ripper's"]], a safehouse captained by [[Akira Kadashi|Akira]], and used by the Red Suns to repair and maintain various technology, including [[Maliwan Corporation|Maliwan]] security bots.
- [[Twilyt Tower]], the corporate headquarters of [[Twilyt Energistics]], a corporation ran and operated by CEO [[Vanzus Starblade]]. 
##### Key Dates:
- `27/06/741 3e`: The Red Suns were repeatedly attacked by [[The Outrunners]], [[Giff Bigstar]] and [[Vermina Sanguinar]] throughout the course of the calendar day;
	- `0700 HOURS`: The Red Suns blockade on [[77th Street]] was assailed by [[The Outrunners]]. One mercenary and three footsoldiers were later found dead at the scene. No civilians were harmed.
	- `1000 HOURS`: The Red Suns safehouse, [[Red Suns Mod Shop|"The Mod Shop"]] was attacked by [[The Outrunners]]. One footsoldier was found dead at the scene, alongside a destroyed [[Maliwan Corporation|Maliwan]]-brand security bot, an injured footsoldier and an injured mercenary (identified [[Taya 'Firebrand' Tayanova|"Firebrand"]]).
	- `1200 HOURS`: A modified civilian gravcar operated by [[The Outrunners]], and a cruising bike carrying [[Giff Bigstar]] and [[Vermina Sanguinar]] left [[Red Suns Mod Shop|The Mod Shop]], heading to the outskirts of [[New Paradise]].
	- `1400 HOURS`: The two vehicles meet outside the [[Red Suns Warehouse]] a few moments before a fight breaks out outside the southern pressure door. Two [[Maliwan Corporation|Maliwan]] security bots were found destroyed alongside the bodies of two brood swarmers, a footsoldier and a codeshaper. The body of a mercenary (identified [[J'rrik 'Beastmaster' Four-Arms|"Beastmaster"]]) was also found at the scene. Obvious interference with the pressure door implies this as the route of entry for [[The Outrunners|the Outrunners]]. 
	- `1430 HOURS`: A second altercation breaks out inside the [[Red Suns Warehouse]], at the site's construction grounds. The bodies of four footsoldiers, three codeshapers and two mercenaries were found at the scene, alongside two destroyed security bots. More casualties are suspected to have taken place, but no evidence of such remains. The aftermath of a large explosion has been observed at the center of the construction site.
	- `1600 HOURS`:  The two vehicles operated by [[The Outrunners]], [[Giff Bigstar]] and [[Vermina Sanguinar]] left the [[Red Suns Warehouse]] grounds, heading back towards [[New Paradise]]. 
	- `1700 HOURS`: These same vehicles are seen parked outside of [[Giff Bigstar|Giff Bigstar's]] armory safehouse. 
	- `1900 HOURS`: [[The Outrunners]] arrive at an [[Freddy Fazbear's Pizzeria|abandoned family diner]], following the tracker trail of [[Akira Kadashi]]. The destroyed remains of a [[Foxy the Pirate|dilapidated animatronic]] and a headless springlock mascot costume were later found at the scene. 
	- `1930 HOURS`: Following the trail of [[Akira Kadashi]] further, [[The Outrunners|the Outrunners]] arrive outside another safe house, known as [[Red Suns Electronics Facility|"The Ripper's"]], and another fight ensued shortly after. The bodies of two footsoldiers, three brood swarmers, two mercenaries and a destroyed [[Maliwan Corporation|Maliwan]] security bot were found at the scene. Alongside these bodies, a [[Abyssal Extract|strange substance]] was also discovered.
	- `2100 HOURS`: [[The Outrunners]], alongside [[Giff Bigstar]] and [[Vermina Sanguinar]] leave [[Red Suns Electronics Facility|"The Ripper's"]], heading towards the [[New Paradise]] Economic district. [[Akira Kadashi]] was brought with them, in handcuffs.
	- `2200 HOURS`: [[The Outrunners]] arrive at [[Twilyt Tower]] in the Economic district, home of the energy company [[Twilyt Energistics]]. A confrontation occurred in the tower's  ground floor lobby. An unconscious codeshaper and footsoldier were found at this scene, alongside the remains of five footsoldiers, two mercenaries and a destroyed [[Maliwan Corporation|Maliwan]] security bot. One codeshaper was unaccounted for at the scene, and has not been seen since the afternoon of the incident. Cameras were cut for this altercation, though some security footage shows the Outrunners walking up to the lobby of this building, alongside [[Giff Bigstar]] and [[Vermina Sanguinar]]. 
	- `2230 HOURS`: Two elevators took off from the lobby of [[Twilyt Tower]] to the 150th floor; the Executive Suite. 
	- `2245 HOURS`: A number of fights broke out as [[The Outrunners|the Outrunners]] swept the 150th floor of [[Twilyt Tower]]. Bodies were recovered in 4 rooms, though investigation believes that this is the result of a drawn-out fight where resources from either side were drawn into the fray gradually. In the Executive Bar, the bodies of two footsoldiers were found alongside a heavily damaged [[Maliwan Corporation|Maliwan]] security bot and a broken guitar. In the corridor over, another Maliwan security bot was found alongside the body of a codeshaper. In the main lobby of the 150th floor, an [[Oozoid]] mercenary (identified [[Taya 'Firebrand' Tayanova|Taya "Firebrand" Tayanova]]) was found, decapitated. In the fire exit corridor, a [[Zorbrak]] mercenary (identified [[J'rrik 'Beastmaster' Four-Arms|J'rrik "Beastmaster" Four-Arms]]) and his pet brood swarmer (identified "Princess") were found, battered against one another. Traces of [[Abyssal Extract]] were found in the Ooze of "Firebrand" while none were found in the blood of J'rrik Four-Arms, however, a full vial of the Extract was found on J'rrik's person.
	- `2300 HOURS`: [[Giff Bigstar]] and [[Vermina Sanguinar]] were seen on camera heading into maintainance floor 149. Footage on the floor itself was not captured, but the destroyed remains of around 50 [[Maliwan Corporation|Maliwan]] security bots were evidence of a scene. It is currently believed that only around 15 of these security bots are still operational within the city of [[New Paradise]] after this operation.
	- `2330 HOURS`: A final altercation broke out between [[The Outrunners]] and [[Twilyt Energistics]] CEO, [[Vanzus Starblade]]. Interestingly, the remains of three abyssal snappers were found in Vanzus' office, while the body of Starblade himself was found on the rooftop terrace. On an investigation of Starblade's body, heavy traces of [[Abyssal Extract]] were found in the [[Nilari|Nilari's]] bloodstream. But just as worryingly, so too were the initial onset symptoms of [[Discordant|Entropic Discordance]]. 
	- `0200 HOURS`: [[The Outrunners]] trickle out of [[Twilyt Tower]]. [[Malalim Dagat]] is the first seen to exit the building. 
- `28/06/741 3e`: [[Twilyt Energistics]] were served a 150 Trillion note lawsuit from the [[Maliwan Corporation]], but with no CEO at the helm of this corporation, its management fell into disarray. [[Kyojin Energy Company]] stepped in, commencing a corporate takeover as they started the process of incorporating Twilyt's assets into their own corporation. In the months since, the Twilyt-owned Rig-Cities of [[Hhecia]], as well as all of Twilyt's properties and assets on [[New Paradise]] and most other affiliated worlds came under the direct rebranding of Kyojin. Despite this, [[Twilyt Tower]] still retains its name as both an iconic symbol of [[Kleio Prime]], as well as in tribute to the strange events of *that night*.
- `MIDYEAR/741 3e` (2 days after [[The Outrunners|the Outrunner's]] attack began): 
	- [[Giff Bigstar]] was formally pardoned for the events of `27/06/741 3e` by 4 different political factions and across numerous sectors, including the [[Cavalon Empire]], [[Solar Federation]] and the [[Argonauts]]. His bounty with the [[Nexus Consortium]] was elevated from "higher priority" to "highest priority". 
	- Records of the involvement of all other [[The Outrunners|Outrunners]], including [[Vermina Sanguinar]] were scrubbed from the investigation. The [[Kuzai Syndicate]] claim that it was their hand that supported the "great" [[Giff Bigstar]], in taking down a "monstrous threat" to [[New Paradise]].
	- A high delegate from [[the Church of Karkosa]] visited the forensics lab where the singular recovered vial of [[Abyssal Extract]] was being analyzed. She confiscated the vial, and formally requested the closure of the investigation. Fearing heightened scrutiny from the Church, and with limited resources to spend on the investigation effort, [[New Paradise]] enforcement closed the case.
