Kleio Prime is the first inhabited and capital planet of the [[Kleio System]], and is the fourth planet from [[Troh and Soh|Troh & Soh]]. An almost barren, desert world, Kleio Prime is home to the Frontier City of [[New Paradise]], which is located along the shores of the [[One Great Sea]].

While the Kleio Prime is considered by many to be a dead-end planet, its galactic importance is held up by its proximity to [[Hhecia]], where an industry has sprung up around harvesting the gas giant for its atmospheric resources. Many of the individuals working at the Hhecian atmosphere rig-cities live in [[New Paradise]] during their off-season.

Solar experts have theorized that the nature of Kleio Prime's barren surface and lackadaisical atmosphere are the direct results of the planet's weak [[World Soul]]. Whether this World Soul in particular is incredibly young, old, or simply just lethargic and listless in life is heavily up to debate. What isn't up to debate is the general feeling of apathy many feel upon setting foot on Kleio Prime.
