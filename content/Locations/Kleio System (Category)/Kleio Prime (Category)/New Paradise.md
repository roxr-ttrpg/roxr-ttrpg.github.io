Also known as the Frontier City, New Paradise is the capital city of the desert planet [[Kleio Prime]], and the [[Kleio System]] at large. Built some 50 years ago along the beaches of the [[One Great Sea]], an entirely landlocked ocean and the planet's singular body of water, New Paradise is the only part of Kleio Prime that is inhabitable to most species in the galaxy.

Under the city's current leadership, redevelopment plans have been scheduled and even started, but disagreements have caused delays and entire cancellations, leading the city to a stagnant state. Thanks to this stagnancy, a group called the [[Red Suns]] has been able to grow in strength, and now threatens the entire balance of the city.
