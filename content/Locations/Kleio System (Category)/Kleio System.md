The Kleio System is one of the many solar systems in the galaxy, named after the capital planet, [[Kleio Prime]]. 
##### Known Celestial Bodies of the Kleio System:
- [[Troh and Soh|Troh & Soh]], the twin red suns at the center of the Kleio System. 
	- [[Kleio Prime]], a desert planet, and the capital planet of the Kleio System, fourth planet from Troh & Soh. 
		- [[Desimus]], the first moon of Kleio Prime. 
		- [[Oibalius]], the second moon of Kleio Prime.
	- [[Hhecia]], an oceanic gas giant home to a number of atmospheric harvesting rig-cities, and the fifth planet from Troh & Soh. 
	- [[Dakta]], a rainforest planet, discovered to be inhabitable after Kleio Prime was settled. Sixth planet from Troh & Soh.
- [[Research Station KlS-01]], (formerly) an interplanetary research facility created and owned by the [[Maliwan Corporation]].
