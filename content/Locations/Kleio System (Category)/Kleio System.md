The Kleio System is one of the many solar systems in the galaxy, named after the capital planet, [[Kleio Prime]]. 
##### Known Celestial Bodies of the Kleio System:
- [[Kleio Prime]], the capital planet of the Kleio System. 
	- [[Desimus]], the first moon of Kleio Prime. 
	- [[Oibalius]], the second moon of Kleio Prime. 
- [[Research Station KlS-01]], (formerly) an interplanetary research facility created and owned by the [[Maliwan Corporation]].
