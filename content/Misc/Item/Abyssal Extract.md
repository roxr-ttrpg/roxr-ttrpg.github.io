Abyssal Extract is a mysterious compound employed by the [[Red Suns]]. The [[Red Suns Warehouse]] location contained an on-site chemlab built specifically with the intention of creating new strains of hard chems laced with Abyssal Extract.

On the same day that [[The Outrunners|the Outrunners]] learned about the existence of Abyssal Extract, they saw it in use as the [[Red Suns]] member, [[Akira Kadashi]], used a Abyssal Extract-based combat stim during an altercation. [[Malalim Dagat]] was the first member of this group to ingest Abyssal Extract, where they experienced strange visions, as if they could see the room they were in from 5 different points at the same time. Shortly after, the Outrunner [[Deez|█████ ████████]] made an attempt at using a vial of Abyssal Extract, but she was instead afflicted with physical pain while a wave of lethargy swept over her.

Later that night, [[The Outrunners|the Outrunners]] witnessed a [[Red Suns]]-affiliated mercenary known as [[Taya 'Firebrand' Tayanova|"Firebrand"]] use a vial of Abyssal Extract, much to the same effect of [[Akira Kadashi]]. Shortly after, [[Malalim Dagat]] used a vial of the Extract and experienced similar effects. After this altercation with "Firebrand", the Outrunners came upon [[Vanzus Starblade]], a [[Nilari]] who claimed he was the one who created, or discovered, Abyssal Extract. Vanzus Starblade also used Abyssal Extract during combat, but with significantly more prowess as the Extract not only enhanced his psionic [[Esper]] abilities, but also provided him with two shadows, rather than the singular ones that Akira, "Firebrand" and Malalim Dagat observed.

It is currently believed that Abyssal Extract's origins are tied to a relic currently in the possession of [[Malalim Dagat]], while the ingredients used are certain vital energies harvested from young and living abyssal specimens.
