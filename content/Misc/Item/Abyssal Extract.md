Abyssal Extract is a mysterious compound employed by the [[Red Suns]]. The [[Red Suns Warehouse]] location contained an on-site chemlab built specifically with the intention of creating new strains of hard chems laced with Abyssal Extract.

On the same day that [[The Outrunners|the Outrunners]] learned about the existence of Abyssal Extract, they saw it in use as the [[Red Suns]] member, [[Akira Kadashi]], used a Abyssal Extract-based combat stim during an altercation. [[Malalim Dagat]] was the first member of this group to ingest Abyssal Extract, where they experienced strange visions, as if they could see the room they were in from 5 different points at the same time.

It is currently unknown what Abyssal Extract is actually made out of, or even what the origins of it are.
