> *Whether enticed by the ease of feeding within heavily populated areas or brooding in the remote reaches of far-flung worlds, the desire of a varg to fill the pit within themselves often leads them to achieve impressive levels of wealth or power—though it is never enough.*
- ***The Outrunner's Handbook, vol 2.***

Varg are one of the galaxy's four [[Discordant|Discordant species]], the ones tempted, twisted or fallen to the [[Discordant Tones|Discordant Tone]] of [[Entropy]]. Entropy is a powerful force, filling afflicted individuals with an emptiness, colloquially referred to by Varg as "the Pit". An incredible yearning, and feelings of overwhelming envy often follow as individuals seek to fill this 'Pit'; typically done by physically draining the emotional energy from the unafflicted, either willingly or unwillingly. 

While Entropic affliction can effect anyone, these properties of [[Entropy]] manifest greatest in the Varg, who have been subjected to them long enough to become twisted from their original species and lineage. In twisting an individual into a Varg, Entropy turns them more and more into a vampiric state; paling and desaturating their skin, growing out prominent fangs, and gaining batlike features in places such as their ears. Additionally, each Varg has at least one patch of fiendish energy, which when gazed into by an onlooker can reveal the emptiness within. 

Like each [[Discordant|Discordant species]], once a Varg has been transformed for long enough they must make a subconscious decision: embrace [[Entropy]] and allow it to transform them fully, or reject it in an attempt to retain their humanity. 
##### Notable Varg:
- [[Vermina Sanguinar]], a Varg revolutionary currently working for [[Giff Bigstar]] as a *Holostar* [[Icon]]. [[Discordant|Discordance]] not hereditary, [[Entropy]] rejected and humanity retained.
##### Varg Homeworlds:
- [[Noxilus]], a planet cloaked in the shadow of an endless night, fully corrupted and considered forever lost to the [[Discordant Tones|Discordant Tone]] of [[Entropy]]. Sleek, dark cities populate the surface of Noxilus, which many undead creatures make their home, in addition to the Varg.
